Place your demo video file here as:
demo.mp4 (full path: frontend/public/videos/demo.mp4)

Once you place the file here:
1. It is accessible at the URL: /videos/demo.mp4
2. The demo page at /demo will automatically detect it and play it.
3. You can also paste an unlisted YouTube or external MP4 URL directly into the Demo Video player on the /demo page.
